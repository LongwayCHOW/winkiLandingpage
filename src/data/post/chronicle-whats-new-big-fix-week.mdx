## 🔔 Notifications — Quiet Competence

Notifications are supposed to help, not haunt. But under the hood, they were doing too much — unnecessary rebuilds, duplicate work, little fires that drained battery and attention.

We cleaned house. Fewer background cycles, no redundant alerts, and smarter coalescing so you get one clear nudge instead of three confusing ones. It’s the kind of fix you won’t see, but you’ll feel in a battery that lasts longer and a lock screen that stays calm.

And for the times you wonder “why didn’t I get a banner?” — we added a clear explanation with a shortcut to system settings. No more guessing.

## ✅ Todo — Smooth Creation, No Ghosts

Adding a todo should feel like writing a sticky note, not filing paperwork. We streamlined the creation flow — fewer taps, less waiting, and the keyboard stays out of your way.

The top‑right menu now fully supports rename and delete. Not tucked away in a submenu — right there when you need it.

And the ghost problem? Gone. Deleting a list used to leave orphaned items floating around. Now it cleans up completely — list goes, items go. Your data stays honest.

## 📋 Property Tabs — No More Hide‑and‑Seek

This one bugged us as much as it bugged you. Swipe over to the Subscription tab and… the selected option would sit half‑hidden, peeking just below the edge. You’d scroll, it’d jump. Annoying.

Now when you swipe to Subscription (or any property tab), the selected option auto‑scrolls into full view. No manual scroll, no guesswork. What you chose is what you see.

## 📅 Calendar — Smoother by the Second

Calendar scrolling had a subtle stutter — fine in isolation, grating over a month of daily use. We optimized rendering paths and animation curves. The result: scrolling that feels buttery, transitions that don’t drop frames, and a timeline that responds as fast as your thumb flicks.

You’ll notice it most on the days when you’re quickly checking what’s ahead — no waiting, no micro‑pause. Just speed.

## 🧹 The Small Ones Matter Too

A polish week isn’t complete without the long tail:

- **Image saving** — no more failed saves or silent errors when you try to keep a photo.
- **Note editing lag** — typing in a long note no longer falls behind your fingers.
- **Asset glow / visual quirks** — those little shimmer artifacts and off‑pixel edges are cleaned up.
- Plus a dozen small fixes across the board that make everyday taps feel more certain.

## What This Means For Your Day

Individually, each fix is small. Together, they change how Chronicle _feels_.

Loading is faster. Taps land where you expect. Notifications help instead of interrupt. The app breathes — and so do you.

This is the Chronicle we want you to use every day: fast, steady, and quiet enough to stay out of your way while keeping your life in order.

### **[A Note from the Developer]**

Before we talk about **Chronicle**, we need to talk about the elephant in the room: **Day One**.

If you journal, you know it. It’s the giant. The "App of the Year" winner. It’s perfect. It’s like a **5-star hotel in the city center**—luxurious, modern, with a concierge (AI) to handle everything, and elevators that glide silently to the top floor.

It is perfect. **But if it’s so perfect, why are people still looking for something else?**

### A Developer’s Preference, Not an Independent Ranking

I develop Chronicle, so this is a personal account of why I wanted a different journaling workflow, not an independent review of Day One.
The hotel and cabin comparison is a metaphor for that preference, not evidence that another app withholds your journal.
For current Day One features, prices, export and subscription terms, consult [Day One’s official site](https://dayoneapp.com/).
For Chronicle’s current scope, see the [Chronicle product page](/homes/chronicle/).

### 🏡 Why Chronicle?

If Day One is the 5-star hotel, **Chronicle is a small wooden cabin in the woods.**

<Image
  src="https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1444858291040-58f756a3bdd6?ixlib=rb-4.0.3&auto=format&fit=crop&w=1024&q=80"
  alt="Cozy wooden cabin in the woods"
  class="rounded-lg shadow-md my-8"
  width={1024}
  height={576}
/>

Let’s be honest. Chronicle is the underdog. I don’t have a massive cloud team. I don’t have AI to analyze your tone. Heck, just yesterday, I was up all night fixing a simple "rotation animation" bug. (Thanks for telling me about it—it’s smooth as silk now! ✨)

**But this cabin belongs to YOU.**

#### 1. It’s Yours, Not Rented.

I don’t want your data. I don’t want to lock you into a forever subscription. Chronicle is built to be **local-first**. This is your corner of the world. You shouldn’t have to pay rent just to visit your own memories.

#### 2. It’s "Enough".

I wanted a clean space to write, a photo that actually loads, and a quiet place to keep my thoughts. That is the design preference behind Chronicle, not a claim that other journaling workflows are wrong.

_We don’t need to stuff everything into a journal. We just need to write._

#### 3. It Has Warmth.

Day One is a tool. Chronicle is a companion.
You won’t shout into the void here. I am right here. Every pixel polished, every bug squashed (like that annoying image preview issue!), is me, a real person, connecting with you.

### Chronicle isn't here to beat Day One.

Day One is for people who want a "Perfect Digital Library."
**Chronicle is for people who want to "Live and Remember."**

We are still building this cabin. It’s not fancy yet. But every plank is laid with care, and the door is always open.

Thank you for being an early resident of Chronicle.
Let's build this home together. 🏡

_—— Longway & The Chronicle Team_
